About Quetta

The historic capital city surrounded by majestic peaks

The Crown Jewel of Balochistan

Quetta, known historically as Little London, sits nestled in a natural river valley flanked by imposing mountain ranges: Chiltan, Takatu, Murdar, and Zarghun. Located at an average elevation of 1,680 meters, it enjoys a distinctive crisp climate, producing premier apples, grapes, and dried fruits.

From ancient trade routes connecting Central Asia to rich cultural heritage and iconic hospitality, Quetta offers an unmatched destination for adventure seekers, history enthusiasts, and food lovers.

  • 🗣️ Languages: Pashto, Balochi, Brahui, Urdu
  • ✈️ Airport: Quetta International Airport (UET)
